## Existing rights and exclusions / Önceki haklar ve istisnalar

Only rights owned by Emin Bulut are licensed here. Third-party material keeps its original
terms, including music, fonts, recordings, dependencies, captures and trademarks. See
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). No noncommercial restriction is added to
independently reusable third-party work.

Learner-facing Arduino/ESP32 snippets remain MIT even inside a lesson, video script, quiz,
translation or mission file with differently licensed content. This covers the sketch and
its code comments, not the surrounding application, artwork or teaching prose. Learners
retain ownership of their original code/data and may use learned ideas and MIT examples in
independent commercial projects. Commercial permission for new restricted material may be
requested from [Emin Bulut](https://github.com/emjnbulut); a request is not approval.

Prior grants remain valid. New notices cannot restrict old material already released under
MIT/CC BY, including unchanged old material inside a later version. Restrictions cover new
copyrightable contributions first offered under the restricted terms. This change cannot
make already-permissively-licensed material commercially exclusive.
