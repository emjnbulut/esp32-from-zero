## English
Title: I ring a doorbell from my phone 🔔 no app, no internet #ESP32 #Arduino
Alt title: How does my phone ring this doorbell with no internet? 🔔 #ESP32 #Arduino

Tap RING on the phone and a doorbell plays on the desk: ding dong. No app, no router, no internet: the ESP32 opens its
own little Wi-Fi network (like a phone hotspot), the phone joins it and opens the board's own web page at 192.168.4.1.
Every button on the page is a link; tap one and the link flies to the board, which rings the buzzer, changes the RGB
LED's color and counts every command on the OLED screen. What would you control with your phone? Tell me in the
comments!
