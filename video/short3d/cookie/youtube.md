## English
Title: Who's stealing my cookies? 🍪 My ESP32 caught the thief #ESP32 #Arduino
Alt title: Could you sneak a cookie past my ESP32? 🍪 #ESP32 #Arduino

Someone kept stealing my cookies, so I built a guard. Arm it, leave the room in 10 seconds, and the moment a warm hand
reaches for the jar, the white dome (an HC-SR501 PIR motion sensor) says "yes": INTRUDER on the OLED screen, a two-tone
siren from the buzzer, and the ESP32 stamps the time like a parking ticket, so when you come back it tells you
exactly when: "2 min ago". What would you guard? Tell me in the comments!
